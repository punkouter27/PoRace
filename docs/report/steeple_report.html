What happened

s1 failed. With a 0.12 m swing target and only the Gaussian tracking term, the policy learned to cross the field slowly (34M: 0/20 steeple, every episode about 0.45 m/s under the command) and then to stand still: from 68.8M it no longer walks at 1.5 m/s even on flat ground (R1 5/10, then 2/10). On rough ground a fall costs more than creeping, and exp(-err^2/0.25) has almost no slope once the error is large, so nothing pulled the speed back up. Training reward still rose (15.2 to 16.4), which is why the reward curve alone looked fine.

s2 changes: a progress term (fraction of the commanded speed made along the command, 0..1, weight 1, steeple only), the original 0.10 m swing target, and a field whose obstacle heights grow from 25 % at the centre to full height at the edge, so every batch mixes easy and hard ground.
