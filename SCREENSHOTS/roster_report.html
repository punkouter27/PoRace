Main menu: pick who races

2026-10-27 · Menu.unity (MenuBuilder) · 540×960 captures from the Windows player via ShotProbe

Behaviour

  • Tap a chip to put that racer on the grid or take them off. The last racer can't be removed.
  • On banked and steeplechase maps, Matt's chip reads “flat only” and can't be picked. If Matt was the only pick, Blue starts.
  • Picks are saved (PoRace.dogMask, PoRace.matt). Autoplay still races everyone.
  • Blue's bodies are the base of the shared MuJoCo model, so when Blue is out it stays in the model, hidden and parked 40 m off the track. Other dogs are removed from the model as before.
  • Headless check: -raceTest -dogMask 10 -matt on Planar Sprint passed all model checks. Gold 68.76 s, Red 71.56 s; Matt DNF (known m6 policy issue).
